# Workshop guide timeline

Open **Rules workshop → Guide timeline**. Choose a faction, turn view and content
types. The chart shows all five phases across your turn and the opponent's turn.
Core steps and activation stages come from the same guide definitions. Rules retain
their saved timing, phase, turn and guide-order hints. The view deliberately skips
roster, detachment and equipment eligibility filters; those restrictions remain on
the expanded cards. Unscheduled and pre-battle records appear in a reference area.

Unit and detachment filters add the catalog entries linked to the timed rules.
Shared rules and datasheets resolve to their original editable record. Archived
source aliases stay hidden. Disabled content is an explicit display option.

Expand a card to read its summary, targets, timing, restrictions and membership.
The pencil button opens a compact editor on the right only when requested. The
timeline uses the full width until then. A successful save closes the editor;
validation and save errors keep the editor and its draft open. It supports rule text,
phase/turn/window, category, applicable unit names, reference status and sequencing
hints; unit stats; detachment notes; and core-step instructions. Saving uses the
existing administrator service and expected revision. It preserves stable IDs,
scope memberships, imported mechanics and unrelated fields, reapplies the catalog,
and updates the timeline and actual guide. Shared edits affect every membership.
Explicit timing edits override legacy built-in placements, and edited imported
abilities receive the stable source link required by guide activation cards.

Hover or keyboard-focus the editor title to highlight its matching cards. Unsaved
edits are guarded when changing views or closing the editor. Search is debounced
and the model is cached by catalog generation and filters. Account changes clear
the timeline and its draft. The offline single-file edition omits this admin tool.

Timeline scale (50–150%) and window-height (320–1,200 px) controls adjust the canvas
without re-rendering or discarding edits. Reset size restores 100% and 640 px.
These display preferences persist on the device. The site's navigation toggle
minimizes the desktop sidebar to an icon rail and hides the mobile navigation row;
its preference persists with the account's local display state. Toggling navigation
also preserves an open timeline draft.

The full-round view assumes your turn is first, matching the displayed round-start
and round-end locations. Event-driven rules still require their exact trigger;
the diagram does not establish that a condition has occurred or that a collection
of units and detachments forms one legal army.
