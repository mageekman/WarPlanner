## Important findings

- **Nurgle’s Gift retains the full menu.** The source encodes aura ranges of 3″, 6″ and 9″ in its first three battle-round entries and a 12″ cap. At Declare Battle Formations, choose one plague for the battle: cover against afflicted ranged attackers plus -1 to their melee Hit rolls; worsened Save; or reduced Move/Objective Control and worsened Leadership, with OC minimum 1. Afflicted enemies also lose 1 Toughness. The old calculator note’s flat Hit-penalty shorthand does not describe the ranged cover branch. Later-round range mapping, leaving-range clearance and dynamic application need confirmation.
- **Ordinary and Maggot Lords scopes remain distinct.** The ordinary army rule covers 30 named units; the Maggot Lords version covers five separate units with its detachment gate. The six allied PLAGUE LEGIONS units do not automatically become base Death Guard emitters. Reverberant Rancidity has a separate 7″ proximity-based grant and +3″ range interaction. No memberships are broadened or merged in this audit.
- **Range and target restrictions matter.** Hypervirulent Strains explicitly caps range at 12″. Plaguesurge targets the on-battlefield Warlord, while its effect does not clearly bind affected emitters. Rabid Infusion requires at least two CHARACTER models; Hidden Amongst the Dead requires unaccompanied reserve POXWALKERS. Attached-unit, not-yet-shot/fought and engagement conditions remain in previews.
- **Fourteen records are empty.** Examples include Blighted Land, Cornucophagus, Death’s Heads (Champions), Eyestinger Storm, Final Ingredient, Grip of the Walking Pox, Leechspore Eruption, Lord of the Walking Pox, Mireslick, Mobile Vector, Needle of Nurgle, Putrid Detonation, Shambling Wall and Tome of Bounteous Blessings. They cannot be completed from names or core phase/CP metadata.
- **One effect differs from the pin.** Parasitic Woe-reaper heals one model in the import while the pinned version says per-model. Original mechanics remain unchanged and the discrepancy is explicit.
- **Mortarion’s shared reaction limit stays visible.** The summary preserves the once-per-turn-per-model gate, 5″ reactive move, 2+ fight-on-death and Ballistic Skill penalty for return fire. Enemy/recipient selection and model-versus-unit eligibility are incompletely bound, so the record remains in review. Undying Spite also checks the wrong not-yet-fought subject.
- **Lord of Contagion resurrection has conflicting limits.** The effect says once per battle per model; the approximate note says first destruction each battle round. The reminder does not pick one as verified. Distance, engagement boundary and destroyed-model binding also need checking.
- **Variants remain separate.** Ordinary Tallyman requires leading for Malicious Calculations; Septimol Fulg’s version does not. Ordinary Plague Marines has contradictory before/after-shooting triggers for afflicted designation; the Maggot Lords version is explicitly after shooting. Death Guard Beasts of Nurgle actually heal to full wounds, rather than changing the Wounds characteristic as the separate Chaos Daemons import does.
- **Other source gaps stay explicit.** These include Typhus’s Eater Plague frequency/target exceptions, Numberless Horde’s unit size and use limits, Sevenfold Chant frequency, dynamic afflicted-state spread, wrong-side Charge penalties, detection range, late movement permissions and cover granted after saves. Pact of Decay’s incomplete faction-forbidden metadata does not justify automatically banning all allied units.

This audit checks coverage and stats against the pin; it does not establish current official points, all weapon profiles/loadout legality or the latest balance update.
