## Important findings

- **Bondsman has no effect record.** Its selection, eligible recipients, range and duration cannot be reconstructed from Duty names. Several Duty abilities activate through self-references; others refer to recipients designated by a particular bearer. Their summaries retain the encoded benefit and explicitly require the missing base procedure. They do not automatically grant the issuing Knight its own buff or buff every Armiger.
- **Code Chivalric is an approximation.** The three Quality menus are retained: Martial Valour, Eager for Challenge and Legacy Unsullied. Nested choices may make simultaneous bonuses into alternatives, Deed victims/control comparisons are incomplete, one destruction comparison is trivially `>= 0`, Honoured state is not defined, and the noted 2/3CP reward is not encoded. Bonus signs, selection and reward rules need checked wording.
- **Additional Oaths are at start of turn.** Heroes of Legend retains no-repeat selection, duplicate-roll handling, persistence of fulfilled Qualities and the additional Deed's 1CP reward, with the source's approximation warning. It appears at start of your turn, separately from start of Command.
- **Valour's Reward references the wrong designation.** Its reset event is `OATH OF MOMENT TARGET`, rather than a defined Code Chivalric event. Crushing Condemnation, Herald of Triumph, Pennant of Silvered Fury and Wyrmslayer Divination mark enhancements expended but lack complete frequency/expended-tag checks; these do not become unlimited-use verified effects.
- **Same names do not establish shared mechanics.** Freeblade Company's Full Tilt has fixed Advance distance 6″, with a prose-only 9″ ARMIGER/DESTRIER branch and 1CP cost. Valourstrike's version costs 2CP and adds 2″ Move and 2 to Advance rolls. The Warden's Thin Their Ranks grants ranged Devastating Wounds against neither-MONSTER-nor-VEHICLE defenders; the separate Spearhead-at-Arms Stratagem is empty. No records are merged or scopes broadened.
- **Thirteen effects are empty.** Acquisitor-at-Arms, Bondsman, Exemplar's Wisdom, Fortress of Intimidation, Hero's Tread, Magos Questoris, Mantle of the Mentor, Martial Tuition, Moment of Glory, Squires of the Hunt, Tales of Heroism, Thin Their Ranks (Spearhead) and Virtue of Courage remain missing. Eight have core Stratagem phase/player/CP metadata, which does not establish their effect. The other five have no timing and remain reference records outside active Guide steps.
- **The Defensive Line is undefined.** Dauntless Defenders and its dependent enhancements/Stratagems reference a region without constructing it. Drive Them Out also encodes Critical Hit value 5 without an unmodified-roll or 5+ threshold. These conditions stay visible and in review.
- **Forgepact allied bindings need checking.** Cogbound Alliance has an unbound 6″ aura origin, a Ballistic Skill sign issue and an incompletely timed 3″/D3 repair. Vengeance of the Machine Cult selects a friendly Adeptus Mechanicus unit in core metadata but tests enemy ownership and marks `this-unit` in the effect. Magos Questoris retains its Tech-Priest bearer restriction and empty effect.
- **Limits and recipients are kept specific.** Flanking Manoeuvres checks 9″ from an edge and not engaged at the end of the opponent's Fight phase. Tactical Foil checks a friendly model within 8″ of the enemy that finished moving. Marshal the Defence retains up to two not-yet-moved units and flags its singular effect binding. Survivor of Strife's Wound penalty requires attack Strength greater than defender Toughness; Titanic Duel tests MONSTER, TITANIC or WALKER with an OR.
- **Upgrade and weapon gates remain intact.** Armiger upgrades preserve their core restrictions, upgrade tags and three-target metadata. Thundercharge requires both named weapons, not a Charge move. Impetuous Glory distinguishes the strike/sweep attack bonuses. Titan Hunter re-rolls Damage, not Wound rolls. Archeotech Autoloaders re-rolls random Attacks-characteristic dice, not Hit rolls. Omnissiah's Grace is mortal-wound-only Feel No Pain.
- **Repair and redeployment windows differ.** Repair Auto-simulacra is at the end of your Command phase; the Iron Chalice is at the end of your Movement phase. Mysterious Guardian is an opponent end-of-turn reminder, separately from end of Fight, and retains its conflicting arrival, ingress and duration details for review.
- **Other unresolved sequences stay explicit.** These include late Super-heavy Walker movement permissions, Martial Pride's undefined engagement-front condition, Ram Jets' move-versus-phase duration, named-weapon suppression victim/expiry bindings, Punishing Salvoes' Movement-only predicate, Selfless Protector granting saves after damage allocation, Crushing Impact's absent rule reference, Thundershock's shared designation scope and Sir Hekhtur's replacement/destruction procedure.

This audit checks source coverage, stored profiles, study summaries and Guide placement. It does not establish current official points, all weapon/loadout legality, allied composition limits or the latest balance update.
