All source units, detachments and ability IDs are represented. Unit profiles validate, native ability links and bodyguards resolve, and every detachment rule/Stratagem/enhancement reference resolves. ## Important findings

- **Waaagh! is provisional.** The imported note says it is carried forward from 10th edition into provisional 11th. The reminder retains Advance re-rolls, riled-up benefits, once-per-battle-per-army declaration and its encoded next-turn duration, but remains in source review. The separate ’Ardmob version remains distinct.
- **Try Dat Button is internally inconsistent.** Its approximate community note and effect table describe different benefits; the note also acknowledges dropped Hazardous escalation. Neither interpretation is promoted into practice.
- **Psychic Level is incomplete.** Waaagh! Energy and Wurrboy contain only no-effect markers. Unstable Energies and Powers of Da Waaagh explain that resource definitions/failure handling need checking. Weapon-only references for ’Eadbanger, Da Krunch and Da Gobshot Thunderbuss do not supply weapon profiles.
- **Bomb Squig is not one interchangeable rule.** Squighog Boyz/Kommandos have one use per battle per unit; Breaka Boyz/Tankbustas have two. Both retain the Normal-move trigger, own Movement phase, visible enemy within 12″, 3+ test and D3 mortal wounds.
- **Four imported variants differ from the pin:** Drive-by Bustin’ (effect and trigger), More Dakka (effect), Paired Krumpas (effect), and Rokkit Charge (scope). Imported versions remain intact and explicitly flagged.
- **Core Stratagem eligibility matters.** Targets retain damage/hit binding, not-engaged restrictions, the 13-model requirement for Mob Mentality, battlefield-edge limits and embark eligibility. Core phase/turn gates drive reminder placement; conflicting effect text remains flagged.
- **Undefined recipients and procedures remain visible.** Examples include Dread ’Ard’s Damage recipient, Grot Shields, the contradictory Infested War Zone selection, detection range, Rapid Disembark, Makari/Grot Orderly tokens and psychic failure branches.
