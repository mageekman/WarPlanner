## Important findings

- **For the Greater Good is incomplete and inconsistent.** Its effect gives +1 Hit and unconditional Ignores Cover against a Spotted target, while its note calls the benefit Ballistic Skill and assumes the Observer sacrifices shooting. Observer/Guided selection, pairing and the MARKERLIGHT gate are missing. It remains in review; no missing procedure is invented.
- **Three records contain no mechanics:** Commander Battlesuit Support System, Sudden Dawn Cadre For the Greater Good, and Sudden Dawn Rapid Disembark. They remain distinct from similarly named working variants.
- **Farsight’s Puretide’s Teachings references a Necron ability.** Its cost-reduction gate requires `my-will-be-done-necrons`; the audit flags that mismatch rather than silently granting the discount.
- **Provisional and partial sources remain explicit.** Examples include Killing Blow, Patient Hunter, Bonded Heroes, several drones and auxiliary rules. Complete-looking numerical effects do not establish that the provisional rule is current.
- **Two source snapshots differ from the pin:** A Tempting Trap’s effect and Targeting Array’s scope. Imported versions are retained and marked for review.
- **Target ownership and identity matter.** Focused Fire has two distinct friendly units and one enemy; Pulse Onslaught has friendly INFANTRY and a hit enemy excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE. Core target restrictions retain not-yet-shot/moved gates, charge reactions and embark eligibility. Core Stratagem phase/turn metadata drives placement; internal effect conflicts stay explicit.
- **Defensive limits and weapon scope stay visible.** Ghostkeel Stealth Drones has two uses per battle per unit; Riptide Nova Charge and Vespid Oversight Drone each have one. Prototype Weapon System chooses one benefit each shooting activation. Weapon Support System ignores ranged Hit modifiers, without silently ignoring Ballistic Skill modifiers too.
- **Several recipients or procedures are unresolved.** Grav-inhibitor Drone and Neocapacitor Shields modify the wrong/unbound Charge recipient; Retro-thrusters sequences two moves; Tidewall Cover triggers after the save decision. Weapon-only drone references lack profiles, and detection range lacks a definition. These issues receive named explanations.
