## Shadow of Chaos scope repair

The stored Shadow of Chaos guide list named 52 units and omitted Poxbringer; the original unit record also has no direct Shadow ability link. As a result, the army rule was absent in the actual guide for a Poxbringer-only list. The pinned core faction index lists Shadow as an army rule, and its effect uses the army-faction condition and friendly LEGIONES DAEMONICA recipients. Every supplied unit has that faction keyword.

The repair clears only this rule’s hardcoded guide `units` list, making the army reminder available to all eligible faction units. Its target text still specifies LEGIONES DAEMONICA and the separate friendly/enemy, region and Greater Daemon conditions. Original imported `unit_ids` and all unit records remain unchanged. A regression test reproduces the missing card before the repair and verifies it appears afterward.

## Important findings

- **Shadow territory is checked at phase start.** The home deployment zone always counts. Controlling at least half the objectives in No Man’s Land adds that territory for the phase; the enemy deployment zone has a separate half-objective test. These are snapshots, not continuously changing territory checks.
- **Shadow recovery branches stay separate.** Friendly LEGIONES DAEMONICA in the Shadow add 1 to Battle-shock; success heals D3 wounds to one non-BATTLELINE model or instead returns D3 destroyed non-CHARACTER BATTLELINE models. Enemies in the Shadow or within the listed Greater Daemon proximity suffer -1 and D3 mortals on failure. The imported one-model recovery differs from the pin, and success/failure effects are nested under before-roll triggers; the rule remains in source review.
- **Fates in Flux is not a working token procedure.** The effect substitutes die values while the approximate note says tokens fund re-rolls. Starting tokens, reciprocal opponent gains/re-rolls and Command-phase regain are omitted. The summary explains the conflict rather than inventing token behavior.
- **Five imported effects differ from the pin:** Death Denied, Foetid Resurgence, Blue Horrors Split, Pink Horrors Split and Shadow of Chaos. Original variants remain intact. Model additions versus new datasheets, recovery recipients and mixed-Horror placement/sequencing need checked wording.
- **Seventeen records have no effect data.** This includes Daemonic Allegiance for Princes, Corrupt Realspace and numerous Scintillating Legion effects. The Soul Grinder’s allegiance is distinct and grants only weapon references without profiles.
- **Core target modes are alternatives.** Archagonists uses one Slaanesh Monster OR up to two non-Monster units; Realm of Chaos uses up to two unengaged units in the Shadow OR one unengaged unit anywhere. The effects do not correctly bind those modes, so they retain review notes. Core phase/turn/CP gates and fallback/engagement/setup restrictions remain in target previews.
- **Defensive and usage differences remain explicit.** Karanak’s Feel No Pain covers Psychic and mortal wounds; Flesh Hounds’ collar covers Psychic only. Shining Aegis and Sheathed in Brass set armour Save to 3, not an invulnerable save. Unholy Vigour grants a 3+ invulnerable save once per battle per unit; Altered Reality is once per battle round; Rider of the Immaterial Winds has a cap not encoded by Hunters from the Warp.
- **Recipient and timing conflicts remain visible.** Examples include Flames of Change’s Advance/Charge penalties, Shade Path, fight-on-death eligibility, cover granted after saves, movement permissions triggered after moves and Realm of Chaos’s Fight-phase/turn-end disagreement. Detection range and cross-faction Dark Pacts procedures are not supplied by these imports.

This audit checks profiles and coverage against the pinned source. It does not establish current official points, every weapon profile/loadout restriction or the newest balance update.
