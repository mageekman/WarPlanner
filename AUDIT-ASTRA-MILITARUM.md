## Important findings

- **Voice of Command has a full menu but unresolved timing.** The summary preserves default 6″ range, officer-specific per-battle-round allowances/recipients, one active Order, expiry at next Command, Battle-shock exclusions and all six options. Take Cover never improves Save beyond 3+; First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire! changes Rapid Fire weapon Attacks. However, the effect uses Command phase-start while its note says during Command, and the extra phase-end disembark/setup condition does not bind the issuing OFFICER. Normal Command reminders use the Command Abilities window and explicitly flag the disagreement; the alternate phase-end procedure needs checked wording.
- **Officer allowances stay distinct.** Lord Solar has three Orders for REGIMENT/SQUADRON/TITANIC; tank commanders have two for SQUADRON; Commissars have restricted menus; Graves mounted/on foot and Drayden’s Lance variants retain different recipients. Master Vox, concurrent-order and self-order interactions remain separate, with source gaps identified.
- **Fourteen records contain no effect:** Calm Under Fire, Coordinated Action, Death Mask of Ollanius, Focus Attacks, Hasty Extraction, Loyal Protector (Ogryn Bodyguard), Order, Over the Top, Reactive Command, Reinforcements, Snap To It, Spec Ops Veteran, Vox Relay, and Vox Relay Beacon. Their names or core CP/phase metadata do not supply missing rules.
- **Three imported snapshots differ from the pin:** Called Shots (scope), Ceaseless Cannonade (scope), and Fire Zone Purge (effect). Imported versions remain intact and flagged.
- **Same-name variants are not interchangeable.** Rogal Dorn Ablative Plating reduces one Damage allocation to zero once per battle per model; the Hammer of the Emperor Stratagem reduces Damage by one for a phase. Ordinary Kasrkin Melta Mine has one use per battle per unit; its Drayden’s Lance variant encodes no usage cap. Slabshield sets Bullgryn Wounds to 4 and Ogryn Bodyguard Wounds to 7.
- **Core target eligibility stays visible.** Fields of Fire requires a REGIMENT and a distinct SQUADRON unit that have not shot. Mordian Minute needs First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire!; No Retreat needs Duty and Honour; Final Hour excludes OFFICER and requires below Half-strength. Flexible Command’s one-or-more selection is retained rather than reduced to one unit.
- **Several mechanics conflict internally.** Masters of Camouflage’s effect grants Stealth while its note describes a conditional Save bonus. Trench Fighters tests the attacking enemy for defending-unit eligibility. Cover rules trigger at Damage allocation after the save decision. Move Out/Scouting Outriders disagree between Fight-phase core metadata and turn-end effects. Reactive movement, redeployment sequences and detection range retain explicit gaps.
- **Provisional source status remains explicit.** Examples include Born Soldiers, Artillery Support, several officer/concurrency rules, resurrection procedures and Aegis deployment. No missing limits or placement rules are invented.

This audit checks profiles and unit coverage against the pinned data, not current points, all weapon profiles, all loadout legality or the newest official balance update.
