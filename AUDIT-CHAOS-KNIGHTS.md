## Important findings

- **Harbingers of Dread retains the full menu.** Deathly Terror is the always-active 9″ Leadership penalty. At the start of rounds 1, 3 and 5, choose one additional ability or roll two D6 for two: Despair, Doom, Darkness, Dismay, Delirium or Dominion. Darkness is Stealth in this source, not the older Hit-penalty branch. The summary preserves cumulative Leadership wording, below-Starting-Strength versus below-Half-strength tests and +3″ Aura range. The odd-round predicate wraps the whole effect, baseline persistence and optional selection do not fully agree, and duplicate/random-choice handling needs checking. Its Guide reminder is at the round opening before the first player's turn.
- **Malefic Surge's options remain alternatives.** The summary retains the Leadership test, D3 failure damage, Empowered duration and single consumption: Unholy Hunger's +3″ Move; Diabolic Power's Lethal Hits OR Sustained Hits 1; Unnatural Fortitude's 5+ invulnerable save OR Feel No Pain 6+. The test/selected-unit binding, Command-only wrapper around later use and after-move trigger need review. Profane Symbiosis retains its non-Empowered and once-per-unit-per-round gates but needs confirmation of out-of-phase activation. Bestial Aspect, Knight Diabolus, Blasphemous Engine and Fleshmetal Fusion keep their option-binding and timing issues visible.
- **Mirror of Fates has incompatible versions.** The effect describes an immediate +1CP tax within 12″ once per turn per model. The note instead describes a once-per-round free Command Re-roll with repeat-use permission and a once-per-battle increase to the opponent's future Stratagem uses. The audit does not pick one as verified or combine them.
- **Storm of Darkness also conflicts.** The effect grants Stealth plus a melee Hit penalty; the note says Stealth plus Benefit of Cover in the opponent's Shooting phase, while core metadata permits Shooting/Fight in either turn. Both interpretations are explained without treating the benefits as cumulative.
- **Two imported differences are preserved.** Pterrorshades heals one model in the import versus per-unit in the pin. Its approximate note says shared healing dice and round frequency are not encoded, although the preserved import supplies both. Titanic Duel's import selects an enemy MONSTER or VEHICLE and upgrades re-rolls of 1 to full Hit/Wound re-rolls against a TITANIC target; the pin selects any enemy and only re-rolls 1s. The source note and core player gate also disagree. This remains distinct from the Imperial +1 Hit/Wound version.
- **Character, upgrade and multi-target restrictions matter.** Flush the Quarry selects a TITANIC Chaos Knight and up to three other War Dogs within 6″, but gives movement permission after moving. Dark Sacrifice excludes Accursed Cultists, keeps the 9″ link and uses one D3+3 roll for damage and healing. Knight Tyrant-only enhancements preserve datasheet restrictions; Octagram preserves its Knight Abominant gate; Hunting Warpack's Executioner/Huntsman upgrades preserve upgrade tags and three-target metadata.
- **Reaction distances are exact.** Encircling Pack requires a War Dog wholly within 12″ of an edge and not engaged at opponent Fight end. Harrying Hounds uses the updated 8″ enemy-distance trigger and up to 6″ Normal move. These are not the Imperial Freeblade edge/reaction procedures.
- **Compound auras need ownership/count gates.** Masters of the Pack's two-War-Dog threshold is absent from its effect, and Throne Tyrannicus has a mismatched designation writer/recipient filter. Marked Prey's visibility is checked during selection without a bound attacker rather than on each beneficiary's attack. Tyrannical Court omits the Warlord/round limit from its free Stratagem effect and has a truncated reference ID. Avenge the Masters has differing tag capitalization and unspecified core target ownership.
- **Detection and suppression need explicit review.** Coursing Thralls, Pterrorshade Rookery and Scenting Fear do not define detection range. Intimidating Reminder applies the penalty to `this-unit` rather than its selected enemy; named-weapon debuffs also have victim/expiry gaps. Unleash Balefire's core not-yet-shot condition conflicts with its after-shooting trigger.
- **Death and movement sequences are not inferred.** Animalistic Rage retains the destroyer-only target, not-yet-attacked gate and pre-Deadly-Demise order from its note, with the broader core player gate flagged. Vox-Howl lacks its friendly recovery and turn limit in the effect. Pitiless Focus expires in Movement despite permitting later shooting. Super-heavy Walker, Trophy Hunter and other movement permissions trigger too late or lack complete paths/end-position rules. Crushing Impact's absent reference is not silently replaced with another Stratagem.
- **Precise ordinary effects stay usable.** Attack-sequence AP protections do not last the whole phase; mortal-only Feel No Pain remains mortal-only; Veil of Medrengard keeps ranged 4+/melee 5+ branches. Seething Hatred is one Hit OR one Wound re-roll, not one of each. Warp Storms keeps a shared once-per-phase-per-army use and checks each enemy once across overlapping emitters. Dreadblades and Wretched Thralls remain composition references rather than automatic list-legality certification.

## Shared chassis and Guide behavior

Eight Chaos Acastus/Cerastus/Questoris chassis have the same named stat profiles as their Imperial counterparts after removing the display name. Their identities, faction keywords and ability references differ; they remain separate unit records. For example, the Chaos Lancer grants War Dogs ranged Assault rather than the Imperial Advance-and-charge Bondsman effect, and the Chaos Castigator's aura grants ranged Sustained Hits 1 without the Imperial Duty's AP branch. Matching chassis stats do not justify merging their full datasheets or granting either faction's rules to the other.
