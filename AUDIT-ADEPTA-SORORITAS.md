## Important findings

- **Acts of Faith is substitution, not an automatic 6.** The source lists Hit, Wound, Save, Advance, Charge, Desperate Escape, Hazard and Leadership rolls/tests. It does not supply a complete generation, eligibility, per-unit/phase usage or multi-die substitution procedure and explicitly does not track the pool. Damage is not silently added to its roll list. Sacred Rites and Ebon Chalice's two-use modifications also lack a fully defined period/base procedure. Stirring Rhetoric and the Macharian Triptych need a bound triggering Act, spent die and substituted roll.
- **Ordinary and Sanctuary Acts of Faith stay distinct.** The ordinary rule covers 33 named units. The separate Sanctuary record covers four variants and has no effect. It cannot be completed by copying the ordinary rule. The other empty records are Blessed Believers and two Sanctuary Simulacrum Imperialis IDs. All four have no timing and stay in reference content outside active Guide steps; matching empty placeholders do not prove identical real rules.
- **Cherub limits are not interchangeable.** Ordinary Battle Sisters/Dominion/Sanctifiers have one use per unit per battle; Retributors have two. Sanctuary Battle Sisters has a once-per-battle name but no encoded usage field. Trigger-to-unit binding is incomplete. The audit preserves every version rather than applying one model's limit to the others.
- **Three imported differences remain explicit.** Sanctuary Extremis Trigger Word lasts a phase in the import versus permanent in the pin; its Hazardous grant also covers melee weapons more broadly than the ordinary flails-only version. Healing Tears specifically returns a Geminae Superia in the import, while the pin returns a generic unit model. Rapturous Blows adds a Palatine-specific successful-Wound trigger and melee gate absent from the pin, but still fails to condition the mortal-wound branch on paying a Miracle die.
- **Miracle-die conservation and frequency matter.** Chaplet's note re-rolls one die or up to three below Starting Strength, while its effect substitutes values and adds a die. Manual of Saint Griselda's up-to-two/sum/cap differs from an exactly-two/unvalued gain. Righteous Rage's up-to-three per-die bonuses differ from a fixed one-die/+1 effect. Solemn Procession's round-based note conflicts with turn-start and every-resource-gain triggers; the audit does not create recursive free sixes. Fiery Conviction retains its spend-a-die OR pass-a-test choice, not both.
- **The full Relics menu is retained.** Select up to two round-long, 6″ effects: Fiery Heart, Censer of the Sacred Rose, Ebon Chalice, Argent Shroud, Valorous Heart or Bloody Rose. Damaged Triumph selection count, Attacks scope/rounding, Acts of Faith periods and overlapping/persistence rules still need confirmation. The Guide does not activate all six.
- **Righteous and Penitent procedures remain conditional.** Righteous Purpose's three-unit menu is missing its designation and several bonuses, with mismatched Leadership/Battle-shock and turn/next-Command fields. Desperate for Redemption preserves all three Vows, the charge gate and 2+ delayed fight-on-death, but needs choice-history confirmation. Boundless Zeal encodes both permissions for both branches despite the non-Penitent OR/Penitent AND note. Mark of Devotion, Shield of Denial, Indefatigable Dedication and Through Suffering retain their distinct conditional escalations.
- **Resurrection is model-specific.** Healing Tears does not return Celestine. Miraculous Intervention preserves first destruction, end of phase, 2+, full wounds and placement details with an unresolved cross-event reference. Divine Intervention excludes Celestine and retains the 1–3-die, wounds/cap, unengaged/unattached and once-per-Character limits, while explaining missing encoded timing/spend/placement restrictions. Sacred Healing and Salvationist Medikit lack complete timing/placement; the latter excludes Characters. Lifewards lacks an explicit named-profile recipient.
- **Transport reactions need correct subjects.** Blazing Ire grants shooting to the transport instead of the selected disembarking unit. Carry Forth the Faithful has incomplete transport/passenger and Normal-move binding. Fervent Devotion lacks explicit move exceptions/turn persistence. Righteous Awareness moves the enemy `event-subject` rather than the Dominion unit. These are review reminders, not automated movement permissions.
- **Fight-on-death and designation references need checking.** Anguish of the Unredeemed and Spirit of the Martyr check the wrong not-yet-fought subject. Denuncia oratory has quarry capitalization/recipient issues; Recount the Deeds uses an inconsistent Agathae tag and unbound casualty branch. The Blood of Martyrs checks an event subject rather than an explicitly bound attacking unit's strength. Junith's Pulpit makes Stealth melee-only, which cannot supply its normal ranged protection.
- **Core target gates stay visible.** Rites of Fire requires disembarkation, not-yet-shot, 6″ and objective range. Suffering and Sacrifice targets Infantry OR Walker units as specified by core metadata. Inspirational Battle Canticles and Shield of Faith keep alternative target modes, not mandatory simultaneous selections; Shield of Faith's effect still needs active-mode/origin binding. Take Cover's Arco exclusion references a keyword absent from the named Sanctuary Arco datasheet, so it remains in review.
